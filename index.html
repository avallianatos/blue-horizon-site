Popular Destinations We Operate

Stingray City & Sandbar

Swim and interact with dozens of friendly southern stingrays in waist-deep, crystal-clear water. The island’s #1 must-do experience.

Starfish Point

Shallow turquoise flats filled with orange starfish. Perfect for photos and gentle snorkeling.

Rum Point

Relaxed white-sand beach, palm trees, and calm North Sound waters. Great for a beach stop or lunch.

Seven Mile Beach

World-famous powdery white sand and turquoise Caribbean Sea. Cruise along or snorkel nearby reefs.

Coral Gardens & Reefs

Vibrant coral reefs teeming with tropical fish — excellent snorkeling stops.

USS Kittiwake Wreck

Famous shallow shipwreck ideal for snorkeling and diving. Explore the artificial reef.
